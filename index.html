How it works
Built around how
relationships actually work
No busywork, no guilt, no complicated setup. Just the people you care about, and a little help remembering them.
01

Add the people who matter

Bring in family, friends, mentors, anyone you don't want to drift from. Choose a relationship type, set how often you want to connect, and Tether starts keeping the rhythm for you.

02

Get a nudge that actually reads the room

See who's overdue and ready to log, who's coming up this week, and when the board is clear with "No one needs a nudge right now." Three different signals, not one blunt alert.

Features
Everything it takes to
actually stay close
Not a CRM. Not another inbox to manage. A quiet companion that pays attention so you don't have to.

A rhythm for every relationship

Weekly, biweekly, monthly, quarterly, annually, or custom. Set the pace that fits each person, not one schedule for everyone.

Never stare at a blank text box

Get ready-to-send openers grounded in what you've shared about someone, then copy a starter and go.

See how every relationship is doing

Open a person and see last talked, cadence, next due, streak, and whether they're on track or overdue, without digging through a long list.

Dates worth remembering

Birthdays and milestones live in one place across your circle, searchable and filterable when you need them.

Turn any date into a reminder, instantly

On desktop, drag an event straight into your reminders. It opens pre-filled with the contact, note, time, and a clear "Reminds you" preview, no retyping, no extra taps.

Reminders that keep watch for you

Pending birthday and event reminders show who, when, and status, so the important dates don't slip.

A monthly report that roots for you

See connections made, what you missed, how you compare to last month, and the size of your circle. No guilt, just the story of how you showed up.

A daily digest in your inbox

Each day, a simple note of who's worth a moment and what's coming up this week, with one tap back into Tether.

Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 23, 2026

1. Introduction

Tether ("we", "our", or "us") is a web application that helps you maintain meaningful relationships by reminding you to stay in touch with the people who matter. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data. By using Tether, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of Tether.

2. Information We Collect

Account Information: email address, display name, first name.

Relationship Data: contact names, relationship types, cadence preferences, interaction logs (type, date, optional notes), birthdays and personal notes. This data is stored solely to power your reminders and relationship health view and is never analyzed for advertising.

Usage Data: basic technical and usage data to operate and improve the service.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and operate the Tether service.
  • To send daily digest emails and reminders you have opted into.
  • To generate AI-powered conversation starters based on your contact notes.
  • To communicate with you about your account.
  • To improve the product based on aggregate, anonymised usage patterns.

4. Third-Party Services

Supabase: used for authentication and database storage, SOC 2 compliant.

Anthropic (Claude API): AI starters can appear when you view a contact who has notes (or a recent interaction note); relevant context is sent to the Anthropic Claude API. Per Anthropic's usage policy, data submitted via the API is not used to train their models.

Stripe: payment processing, Tether never stores your credit card information directly.

Brevo: used to deliver daily digest emails and transactional notifications.

5. AI-Powered Features

AI conversation starters can appear when you view a contact who has notes (or a recent interaction note). We use the Anthropic Claude API and send relevant context (contact name, relationship type, notes) to generate suggestions. Per Anthropic's terms, data sent through their API is not used to train their models. Suggestions are AI-generated and are not guarantees—they may occasionally be inaccurate or feel off; use your judgment.

6. No Sale of Data

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party for marketing or commercial purposes. Ever.

7. Data Storage and Retention

Your account data is retained for as long as your account exists. If you delete your account, your personal data and relationship records will be permanently deleted within 30 days. You can permanently delete your account at any time from Settings, under Account. If you cannot access your account, email hello@staytether.app for assistance.

8. Your Rights

Access: download a copy of the personal data we hold, from Settings under Privacy and data.

Correction: update your information directly in app settings.

Deletion: permanently delete your account and all associated data from Settings, under Account.

Portability: export your relationship data as a JSON file from Settings, under Privacy and data.

Most of these are available directly in Settings. For anything else, or if you cannot access your account, contact hello@staytether.app.

9. Children's Privacy

Tether is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

10. Security

Industry-standard security measures including encrypted data transmission (HTTPS/TLS), secure authentication via Supabase Auth, and row-level security on our database. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the effective date at the top of this page. Continued use of Tether after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

12. Contact Us
