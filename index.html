How it works
Simple enough to
actually stick to
Three steps. No complicated setup. Just you and the people you care about.
01

Build your circle

Add the people who matter: family, close friends, mentors. Set how often you want to be in touch with each one.

02

Get gentle reminders

Tether checks in daily and lets you know who is due for a call, text, or coffee. No guilt, just a nudge.

03

Log and move on

Reach out, log the interaction in two taps, and let Tether reset the clock. The app does the tracking so your brain does not have to.

Features
Designed around
real relationships
Not a CRM. Not a task manager. Just a thoughtful companion for staying connected.
💚

Relationship health view

Color-coded indicators show at a glance who is thriving in your circle and who is slipping, before it has been too long.

✨

AI conversation starters

Not sure what to say after a while? Tether generates warm, personal openers based on what you know about the person.

📅

Custom cadences

Weekly for your parents. Monthly for close friends. Quarterly for mentors. Set the right rhythm for each relationship.

📬

Daily digest emails

A gentle morning email showing who is due today, with one-click logging so you never have to open the app if you do not want to.

🎂

Birthday & custom reminders

Add birthdays, anniversaries, or any date that matters. Tether reminds you ahead of time so you never miss a moment worth acknowledging.

📊

Monthly relationship report

A personal digest of who you showed up for, who you missed, and what patterns are forming in how you stay connected.

Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 10, 2026

1. Introduction

Tether ("we", "our", or "us") is a web application that helps you maintain meaningful relationships by reminding you to stay in touch with the people who matter. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data. By using Tether, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of Tether.

2. Information We Collect

Account Information: email address, display name, first name.

Relationship Data: contact names, relationship types, cadence preferences, interaction logs (type, date, optional notes), birthdays and personal notes. This data is stored solely to power your reminders and relationship health view and is never analyzed for advertising.

Usage Data: basic usage data to understand how the app is used and enforce free-tier limits.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and operate the Tether service.
  • To send daily digest emails and reminders you have opted into.
  • To generate AI-powered conversation starters based on your contact notes.
  • To communicate with you about your account.
  • To improve the product based on aggregate, anonymised usage patterns.

4. Third-Party Services

Supabase: used for authentication and database storage, SOC 2 compliant.

Anthropic (Claude API): when you request AI conversation starters, relevant contact notes are sent to the Anthropic Claude API. Per Anthropic's usage policy, data submitted via the API is not used to train their models.

Stripe: payment processing, Tether never stores your credit card information directly.

Brevo: used to deliver daily digest emails and transactional notifications.

5. AI-Powered Features

Our AI conversation starters use the Anthropic Claude API. When you request a starter for a specific contact, we send relevant context (contact name, relationship type, recent interaction notes) to Anthropic's servers to generate the suggestion. Per Anthropic's terms, data sent through their API is not used to train their models. We do not store the AI-generated content; it appears in your app only when requested. Suggestions are AI-generated and may occasionally be inaccurate or feel off; use your judgment.

6. No Sale of Data

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party for marketing or commercial purposes. Ever.

7. Data Storage and Retention

Your account data is retained for as long as your account exists. If you delete your account, your personal data and relationship records will be permanently deleted within 30 days. To request account deletion, email hello@staytether.app with subject "Delete My Account".

8. Your Rights

Access: request a copy of the personal data we hold.

Correction: update your information directly in app settings.

Deletion: request permanent deletion of your account and all associated data.

Portability: request an export of your relationship data.

Contact hello@staytether.app to exercise any of these rights.

9. Children's Privacy

Tether is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

10. Security

Industry-standard security measures including encrypted data transmission (HTTPS/TLS), secure authentication via Supabase Auth, and row-level security on our database. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the effective date at the top of this page. Continued use of Tether after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
